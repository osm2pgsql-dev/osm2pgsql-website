title: Happy Birthday osm2pgsql!
---

Today 20 years ago Jon Burgess committed the first version of osm2pgsql to the
repository. Since then osm2pgsql has been improved in many ways by many people.
Originally started to enable rendering of OSM data into raster tiles, it has
now many more uses. It is still the software behind many of the most important
(raster or vector) OSM maps, especially when minutely updates of the data are
needed.

If you want to know more about the history of the project, our website has
an [overview](https://osm2pgsql.org/about/history/). We also showcases some
of [our users](https://osm2pgsql.org/about/users/) there.

We keep working on osm2pgsql, some ideas for that are on our [road
map](https://osm2pgsql.org/about/road-map.html), but we always like to hear
about your needs and ideas, too.

Osm2pgsql is free to use and always will be. But it is not free to make.
[Please support us in any way you can](https://osm2pgsql.org/sponsors/).
